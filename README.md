# ☕ Starbucks Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Starbucks Sales Analysis Dashboard developed using Power BI. The objective was to transform raw sales transaction data into meaningful business insights that support data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, and payment preferences through interactive visualizations and KPIs.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall sales performance and order trends.
* Identify peak sales hours and customer purchasing patterns.
* Discover top-performing products across different periods.
* Evaluate customer payment preferences.
* Compare customer segments and their contribution to revenue.
* Deliver actionable business insights through an executive dashboard.

## 📈 Key Insights

### ⏰ Peak Sales Performance

Morning hours generated the highest sales activity, indicating strong customer demand during breakfast and early working hours.

### ☕ Product Performance

Top-performing products consistently contributed a significant portion of overall sales and maintained strong rankings across multiple months.

### 💳 Payment Preferences

Digital payment methods emerged as the preferred transaction mode, highlighting customer inclination toward cashless payments.

### 👥 Customer Segmentation

Customer segments contributed differently to overall sales performance, enabling a deeper understanding of customer behavior and engagement.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Increase staffing during peak sales hours to improve service efficiency.
* Promote lower-performing products through targeted offers and bundles.
* Strengthen digital payment incentives to encourage customer adoption.
* Enhance customer loyalty initiatives through mobile app engagement.
* Optimize inventory planning based on product demand patterns.
